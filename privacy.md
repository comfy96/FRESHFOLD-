# Fresh Fold Laundry Privacy Notice

**Last Updated:** November 2026  
**Applicability:** Customers, counter patrons, and delivery users across Nigeria  
**Governing Law:** Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023  

---

## 1. What Fresh Fold Is and What We Believe

Fresh Fold Laundry is a neighbourhood laundry and dry-cleaning service. We are in the business of washing, pressing, and delivering clean clothes—not harvesting, tracking, or selling personal data.

We collect only the bare minimum information necessary to track your garments, contact you when your order is ready, and prove what was received and paid. Our portal is built around one straightforward rule: **you have complete visibility into your own item counts, docket records, and payment receipts, and no customer can ever see yours.**

---

## 2. What Information We Collect & Why

We collect six pieces of information when you register or drop off laundry:

1. **Your Name:** Written on your laundry intake tag so our shop floor washers and ironers don't mix up your garments.
2. **Your Nigerian Mobile Number:** Used strictly so our dispatch riders (like Musa) can call or WhatsApp you when arriving for a pickup or delivery. We never send unsolicited marketing SMS.
3. **Your Email Address:** Used to log into your account, send 6-digit security codes, and deliver your dated receipt summaries.
4. **Your Counter Docket Number:** The sequential number printed on your physical receipt slip, linking your digital account to your laundry bag on our shelves.
5. **Your Security Credentials (Password & Claim PIN):** Stored only as irreversible cryptographic hashes (Argon2id and SHA-256). No Fresh Fold staff member or system administrator can ever see or read your actual password or PIN.
6. **Payment Confirmation Numbers:** When you pay via POS or bank transfer, we record the payment amount, date, and transaction reference to settle your bill. We never store credit/debit card numbers on our servers.

---

## 3. How Long We Keep Your Information

We do not hold onto your personal records longer than necessary:

- **Active Laundry Records:** Maintained while your clothes are being processed and for 12 months afterward to resolve any questions about fabric care, claims, or repeat orders.
- **Login Sessions:** Active for 7 days. When you log out, your session is immediately deleted from our database.
- **Financial & Tax Records:** Retained for six (6) years as required by Nigerian law (Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020 and Federal Inland Revenue Service tax regulations).
- **Temporary Security Codes:** 6-digit email confirmation codes expire and are deleted within 7 minutes.

---

## 4. Your Rights & How to Delete Your Account

Under Section 34 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to request the complete erasure of your personal data.

You can request account deletion at any time directly through your account settings or by emailing `records@freshfold.ng`.

### What Happens When You Request Deletion
1. **Immediate Lockout:** Your account is closed immediately, and all active sign-ins across all your phones and laptops are revoked.
2. **7-Day Grace Period:** We pause for 7 days in case the request was made by mistake or your account was compromised.
3. **Permanent Scrambling:** After 7 days, our automated system permanently anonymizes your record. Your name, email, phone number, and password hashes are permanently erased and replaced with generic labels (such as `Purged Customer #405`).
4. **Lawful Payment Preservation:** Past financial payment transactions remain in our financial ledger to satisfy tax accounting laws, but they will never again be linked to your personal name or phone number.

---

## 5. Who We Share Data With

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only with:
- **Delivery Riders:** Shared on a per-trip basis (your delivery address, name, and phone number only for active drop-offs).
- **Email Service Providers:** To deliver your security codes and receipts over secure encrypted connections (TLS).
- **Law Enforcement & Tax Authorities:** Only when required by a valid court order or statutory tax audit under Nigerian federal law.
