# Terms and Conditions Source Note (`docs/terms-note.md`)

## Where the Wording Came From
The plain-language terms summary shown above the acceptance checkbox in `src/app/auth/AuthForm.tsx:391-396` was adapted from standard Nigerian Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023) small-business consent checklist templates and stripped down into everyday English tailored to Fresh Fold Laundry:

> *"Fresh Fold Laundry stores your name, phone number, email, and garment counts solely to show your dated laundry and payment records. Financial records are retained for Nigerian tax audits; contact Chinedu Okafor on 0803 000 4120 for record requests."*

## Honest Legal Disclaimer
We did **not** ask an AI agent to invent full legal terms from scratch and ship them blindly as binding law, nor am I a lawyer. An agent generates fluent legal boilerplate that frequently contradicts actual system behavior (for instance, promising instant erasure of payment ledgers that Nigerian tax law requires keeping, or naming front-desk procedures the software cannot process). Before commercial launch, this adapted summary and the full `privacy.md` policy (Task 05) must be reviewed by qualified legal counsel.
